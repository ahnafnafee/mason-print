Printer filters now have an explicit confirmation button, so you can see and apply your choices before returning to the list.

- Choose a building and floor, then tap **Show N printers** to apply the filter. The button shows how many printers match and stays above the keyboard.
- Pressing Back cancels your edits, including a pending Clear, and preserves the previous filter.
- Searching brings matching buildings forward. Selecting a building dismisses the keyboard and reveals the floor choices; selecting the same building again keeps your chosen floor.
