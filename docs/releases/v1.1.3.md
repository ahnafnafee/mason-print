Uploading documents now takes you straight back to the print queue, with progress where you expect to see the new jobs.

- **Stay in the queue.** The separate “Upload documents” screen is removed. Picking or sharing files shows compact progress above the queue, followed by a short confirmation when uploads succeed. Canceling the picker leaves you in the queue.
- **See which files need attention.** Refused or unconfirmed documents appear in a dismissible notice with expandable details. Refreshing the queue no longer erases an upload error. If a response is lost, the app asks you to check the queue before uploading again.
- **Keep incoming shares.** A second batch shared while an upload is running waits its turn. Fixed a race that could otherwise discard those pending documents, and disabled deletion during other queue work.
- **Improve accessibility.** Upload has an explicit accessibility label, and progress and error text wrap on narrow screens with larger text settings.
