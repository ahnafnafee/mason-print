Find campus printers by recognizable GMU building names and street addresses instead of relying on abbreviated station codes.

- Building names and available addresses appear in the printer list, building filter, starred and recent printers, and release confirmation.
- Search by building name, address, room, or a supported former building name. Original printer labels remain visible so you can match the sticker on the device.
- Added mappings across 302 printer records and 80 location groups, including **FSB → Fuse at Mason Square** and **ACT → Activities Building**. Buildings with the same abbreviation on different campuses are distinguished.
- Inferred locations and unverified addresses are labeled. Entries without a resolved address do not show an invented one, and these GMU mappings do not replace another print server's location labels.
