This update fixes mixed-document print settings and makes the queue, printer selection, and document preview easier to use.

- **Edit several documents reliably.** Changing colour, sides, or copies preserves settings you did not edit on each document. Mixed selections show mixed values, and server-locked settings are identified on the affected job. Cost estimates also preserve each document's own settings.
- **Read the queue at a glance.** Job cards separate document names, estimated prices, print-setting labels, and Preview actions. Colour printing and restricted settings have distinct visual labels. The shared Pay with banner makes the selected funding source clear.
- **Zoom into PDFs.** Pinch to zoom up to 4×, pan enlarged pages, or double-tap to zoom and reset. Zoom and Fit buttons provide the same controls without gestures.
- **Choose a printer before releasing.** Scanned and typed codes lead to confirmation for the selected jobs. The camera viewfinder is square, and code-entry buttons wrap on narrow screens or with larger text.
- **Review a complete estimate.** Confirmation lists the selected documents, their settings, and requested funding source. Release waits for a complete, successful quote rather than accepting a partial or unknown estimate.
- **Recover more clearly.** Improved shared-file handling, queue refresh and pagination, keyboard access, preview errors, and embedded Print Center retry actions. Sign-out and stored-session cleanup are more reliable.

Server restrictions still apply: a setting the print server locks cannot be overridden by the app.
